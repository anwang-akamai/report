ES Health Q2 vs Q3 2026 TAT/TTFB Trends

Executive Summary

  • Global FF TAT increased 2.75% (9.41 to 9.67), while ESSL TAT decreased 1.92% (14.24 to 13.97).
  • Global TTFB increased for both networks: FF +12.38% (240.38 to 270.15) and ESSL +7.32% (244.77 to 262.68). Since lower latency is better, both are degradations.
  • The largest continent-level TAT increase was FF South America (+31.62%); the largest TTFB increase was FF Europe (+24.43%).
  • At country level, TAT rose most for FF Brazil (+31.62%) and ESSL India (+20.97%). TTFB rose most for FF Italy (+28.07%) and ESSL India (+17.23%).
  • By HTTP version (unweighted means across 15 countries per network), HTTP/1 TAT improved for FF (-2.96%) and ESSL (-10.26%), while TTFB rose for both (+3.59% and +5.70%). HTTP/2 degraded across all four metric/network pairs, led by FF TTFB (+31.82%). HTTP/3 was mixed: TAT rose for FF (+11.20%) but fell for ESSL (-25.54%); TTFB fell for FF (-1.63%) but rose for ESSL (+12.63%).

Scope and Caveats

P95 only Q2 2026 vs Q3 2026, TAT (r83 write_delay) and TTFB, FF and ESSL. Lower latency is better.
